Ovarian Drilling for PCOD Treatment ...

Are you struggling with irregular periods, difficulty conceiving, or PCOS/PCOD symptoms despite a healthy lifestyle and medications? At Nakshatra Clinic in Pune, we offer Laparoscopic Ovarian Drilling (LOD), a minimally invasive surgical treatment designed to restore ovulation, rebalance hormones, and improve fertility outcomes.

Our expert team provides **personalised PCOS/PCOD care in Pune**, ensuring treatment plans are tailored to your unique body and health goals. With compassionate guidance, advanced surgical techniques, and fertility-focused support, we help women take confident steps toward achieving their dream of motherhood.

How the Doctor Performs Laparoscopic Ovarian Drilling

Step 1: Anesthesia & Access

The procedure is performed under general anesthesia. Small, keyhole incisions are made in the abdomen for laparoscopic access.

Step 2: Pelvic Assessment

The doctor inspects the pelvic organs, ovaries, and fallopian tubes to rule out other conditions like endometriosis or adhesions.

Step 3: Ovarian Drilling

Using controlled diathermy, the doctor creates a few tiny punctures on each ovary’s surface. This helps reduce androgen-producing tissue and restores hormonal balance, promoting ovulation.

Step 4: Completion & Recovery

  • Duration: Typically 30–60 minutes.
  • Most patients go home the same day or next day.
  • Light activities can usually resume within a few days.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Ovarian drilling is a minimally invasive laparoscopic procedure for women with PCOD who struggle with ovulation. It uses precise heat energy to make small punctures on the ovary’s surface, reducing androgen levels and improving the chance of natural ovulation and pregnancy.

The surgery is done under general anesthesia using laparoscopy. A small incision is made in the abdomen, and a laparoscope with a tiny camera is inserted. The surgeon then uses controlled heat to make small punctures on each ovary to restore normal ovulation.

Since it’s performed under general anesthesia, patients don’t feel pain during the procedure. After surgery, mild abdominal discomfort or shoulder-tip pain may occur due to the gas used in laparoscopy, but it usually resolves within a few days with prescribed medication.

Ovarian drilling helps restart ovulation, reduces excess androgen production, and can increase the chances of natural conception. It also decreases the need for high-dose fertility medicines, making it a good option for women who have not responded to first-line PCOD treatments.
